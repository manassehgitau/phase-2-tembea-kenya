Travel tips

  • Carry cash for park gates and small towns. M-Pesa is accepted almost everywhere, but a card is not.
  • Book coastal stays well before the December and August peaks — the good places fill months ahead.
  • Pack layers for the highlands. Nairobi and Mount Kenya get genuinely cold at night, whatever the equator suggests.
  • Ask before photographing people. It costs nothing, and it is the difference between a snapshot and a welcome.
