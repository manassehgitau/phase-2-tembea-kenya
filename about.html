About Tembea

Tembea started in 2021 in a one-room office on Ngong Road, after our founder spent three years watching visitors arrive in Kenya with itineraries built entirely from search results. They saw the same four places, in the same four days, and left believing they had seen the country.

We do one thing: we ask what kind of trip someone actually wants, and then we tell them the truth about where to find it. Sometimes that is the Mara in August. Often it is somewhere they had never heard of, in a month they had not considered, for half the money.

Every destination on this site has been visited by someone on our team, and every guide we recommend lives in the area they guide. We are not an agency and we do not run tours. We plan, we introduce, and then we get out of the way.

The best trip is not the one with the most stops. It is the one where you stayed long enough that somebody remembered your name.

— Asha Wanjiru, founder

Frequently asked questions

Do you book flights and hotels for me?

No. We plan the route, recommend places to stay, and introduce you to guides. You book directly, which means you pay the real price rather than a price with our margin inside it.

How far ahead should I get in touch?

Three months for July to October and for December, since coastal properties and Mara camps fill early. Three to four weeks is usually enough for the rest of the year.

Is Kenya suitable for a first solo trip?

For most travellers, yes. Domestic flights and long-distance buses are straightforward, and the parks and coastal towns are used to solo visitors. We will be honest with you if a specific plan is a bad idea.

What does a Tembea plan cost?

The first conversation is free. A full written itinerary is a one-off fee agreed before we start, and it does not change based on what you then choose to book.
